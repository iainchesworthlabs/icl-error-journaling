# FAQ

Short answers. Each one links to the Guide, which has more.

## Buying and availability

**Is there an Android version?**
: Not at the moment. Field Test is for iPhone and iPad.

**Is there a subscription?**
: No. It is one purchase of 6.99 USD, with no in-app purchases. The price in your country is shown in the App Store.

**Can I try it first?**
: There is a [sample setup file](index.md#try) with made-up names. It shows how a board looks. The app itself is a paid download.

## Logging

**Why does one tap do nothing?**
: A tile logs on a double-tap, so a touch by accident never makes an entry. One tap shows the hint "Double-tap to log". See [A day of logging](guide/logging.md#log-an-error-double-tap).

**I logged the wrong error.**
: Tap Undo within five seconds. After that, open Review: tap the entry to change its error, or swipe it left and tap Delete. See [Sharing and the next day](guide/sharing.md#review).

**How do I add a note?**
: Hold the tile for half a second, or type in the note field and tap Add. See [Notes](guide/logging.md#notes).

**The error I need is not on the board.**
: Tap Other. It lists every error type, with search. You can add a new one there. See [Other](guide/logging.md#other-errors-that-are-not-on-the-board).

**The software changed during the day.**
: Change the software version in Today's details and choose "It changed during the day". Earlier entries keep the old version. See [Today's details](guide/todays-details.md#when-the-software-version-changes).

**Can I log without a connection?**
: Yes. The app does not use the network.

## Sharing

**What does the shared log look like?**
: A header, then one line per entry. See [How to use](how-to-use.md).

**Does copying count as sharing?**
: No. Only Share log does.

**What happens to yesterday's log?**
: It stays in History. See [History](guide/history.md).

## Your data

**Where are my logs?**
: On your device only, until you share one.

**Does the developer see my logs?**
: No. The app sends nothing, and the developer has no way to look.

**How do I delete a log?**
: In History, swipe left and tap Delete. A deleted log cannot be brought back.

**Is it backed up?**
: Your device's own backup, if you have one switched on, includes the app's files. That is under your account. See [Privacy](privacy.md).

The full list is in the Guide: [Questions and answers](guide/faq.md).
