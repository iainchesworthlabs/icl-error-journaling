# Privacy

Last changed: 14 November 2026.

**Field Test collects no data about you.** It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting,
and it does not send anything over the internet. This page says what that means in practice.

## What the app stores

- **Your logs.** Each day's entries, the notes you type and the day's details (unit, place, software versions (the robot's and any others you add), date and
  your name as the facilitator).
- **People you name.** Only if you turn on the second board and choose to type a name (see below).
- **Your setup.** The error types, places and note templates you set up, and your preferences, such as the time format.
- **A few settings of the app itself**, such as whether you have closed the tip on the Log screen, whether the optional second board is on, which board you used last, how names are written in a shared log and the label
  of an interaction line. They are a switch and a word in a small file of their own; they are not in your logs and not in a setup file.

## People you choose to name

If you turn on the second board (Interactions), you can keep a short list of people, so that you type a name once and pick it
later. The list is yours: the app never fills it in, and a log that names nobody has no names in it.

- **Where the names are.** In a file of their own in the app's storage, and in the app's database. A log's own record holds
  only a number for each person, never the name, so a name you remove is gone from the app (Settings, People, Forget).
  Entries that named that person stay and then read "someone".
- **What leaves the device.** Only what you share or copy, at the level of detail you choose in Settings (the default is
  initials: "Alex Sample" is written "A.S."; the other choices are the full name and "someone"). Share, Copy and Copy
  log all follow that choice, also for a log you share again later. Review and the board show the full name, because they
  stay on the device. The setup file you can export holds no people, and there is no way to export the list itself.
- **Your device backup** holds the list with the rest of the app's files, as described below. Forgetting a person does not
  change a backup that was made before.
- The developer receives none of it: the app sends nothing over the internet.

## The microphone

Field Test uses the microphone for one thing: dictating a note. Nothing listens until you tap the microphone in the note
field, and it stops when you tap it again, after a minute of silence, or when you leave the app. The first time, the
phone asks you to allow the microphone and speech recognition. The words are recognised by your phone, on the phone:
no sound and no text are sent to the developer, to Apple, to Google or to anyone else, and the sound is not recorded or
kept. If your phone cannot recognise speech on the phone for your language, the microphone is not shown. You can turn
the permission off at any time in the phone's Settings; typing keeps working.

## Where it is stored

Only on your phone or tablet, in the space the system gives the app. Other apps cannot read it. On iPhone and iPad the
files use the system's data protection, so they stay locked after a restart until the device is first unlocked.

## What leaves your device

Only what you choose to send, to where you choose to send it:

- **Share.** When you share a log, the system's share sheet opens and you pick where it goes (a chat app, email, Files).
  The developer does not receive it.
- **Copy.** When you copy a log, it goes to your clipboard.
- **Export a setup file.** The file goes where you save or send it.
- **Open a link.** The app has links to the project's pages (this site and the source code). A link opens in your
  browser, which then makes its own connection. The app itself makes none.

## Your device backup

The app's files are part of your device's normal backup, if you have one switched on: iCloud or a computer backup on
iPhone and iPad, Google's backup on Android. That is your own backup under your own account, and the developer cannot see
it. If you do not want your log in a backup, switch the backup off for the device or delete the log in the app.

## Deleting your data

- **A day's log:** delete it in the app (History, then the log).
- **Everything:** delete the app. Its logs, setup and settings are deleted with it. A copy of the data that is in a device
  backup stays there until that backup is replaced or deleted.

## Children

The app is for people who run robot sessions at work. It is not aimed at children and asks for no personal details.
