<section class="ft-text">
<h2>The app</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Text size.</strong> The app follows your device's text size setting.</li>
<li><strong>Light and dark.</strong> The app follows your device's appearance.</li>
<li><strong>Touch targets.</strong> Buttons and rows are at least 44 points high.</li>
<li><strong>Not colour alone.</strong> Each tile carries a label as well as a colour.</li>
</ul>
<h2>This site</h2>
<ul>
<li>These pages work without JavaScript. The Guide's search needs it.</li>
<li>They follow your device's light or dark setting.</li>
<li>The site's colours are checked against the WCAG 2.2 AA contrast levels when the site is built.</li>
</ul>
<p>If something does not work for you, tell us on the <a href="../support/">Support</a> page.</p>
