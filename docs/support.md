# Support

CS Bingo Board is an app for logging robot errors during a session. This page says where to get help.

## Before you ask

The [home page](index.md) links to the guide as it is written. Most questions about setting up the board, logging and
sharing are answered there.

## How to get in touch

Open an issue on the project's page:
[github.com/iainchesworthlabs/icl-error-journaling/issues](https://github.com/iainchesworthlabs/icl-error-journaling/issues).
You need a free GitHub account to do that.

## What to tell us

- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
- The app version and the commit. Open **Settings**, then **About CS Bingo Board**; the **This build** group lists both.
- The kind of phone or tablet and its system version.
- A screenshot, if it helps.

**Do not send a real log or a real setup file.** They name your sites, units and people. If a log or setup is needed to
show a problem, make a small example with made-up names first.

## What the app does not do

The app has no accounts and sends nothing from your device, so nobody on the project can see your logs or look up your
setup. If you lose a phone, the log is on the phone and in the phone's own backup, if you have one. See
[Privacy](privacy.md).
