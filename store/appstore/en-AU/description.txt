Field Test is an error log for people who run sessions with a robot.

Each common error has its own tile on the board. Double-tap the tile and the error goes into the day's log with the local time. Notes, templated notes and changes to the day's header, such as a new software version, go into the same journal, in time order.

At the end of the day, share the log as plain text through the share sheet: to a chat app, Mail, Files or the clipboard.

ON THE BOARD
• Up to 11 error tiles plus Other, each with its own colour and a running count
• Undo for a mis-tap
• Free-text notes, and note templates for the things you write every day
• Dictate a note: tap the microphone and speak, where the phone can recognise speech without the internet. Recognition happens on the phone, so no sound or text leaves it

OBSERVATIONS, IF YOU WANT THEM
Observations is an optional second board. It is off until you switch it on in Settings. Use it for what you did around the unit as well as what went wrong. Make your own tile types, such as "Spoke with [person]", and pick the people from a short list you keep on the device. Names are kept in their own store, never in the log or a setup file, and a shared log writes them as initials unless you choose otherwise.

THE DAY'S LOG
• A header with the tester, date, unit, number suffix (such as UNIT-27-prod), location and robot software version
• Other versions, such as a phone app, with a name and number of their own
• Changes to the header during the day are logged with their time
• Review, edit, copy or delete any entry
• Earlier days kept in History

SET UP YOUR WAY
• Set up the board by hand, or import a setup file shared from a Mac or another device
• Light and dark appearance, and larger text
• On iPad, the board and the log side by side in landscape

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Field Test has no account, no network access, no analytics and no advertising. Your log stays on your device, protected by the device's data protection, and leaves it only when you share it.
