WHAT THE APP IS FOR
CS Bingo Board is a log for a facilitator who runs sessions with a robot. During a session the facilitator taps a tile each time the robot makes a known kind of error. The app records the error with the local time. At the end of the day the facilitator shares the day's log as plain text, usually to a team chat. "Bingo board" means the grid of tiles. There is no game, wager, score or prize.

NO ACCOUNT, NO NETWORK, NO HARDWARE
There is no sign-in and no demo account is needed. The app has no network access and no server. It does not connect to the robot or to any device. All data stays on the device and leaves only through the system share sheet or copy, when the user chooses.

HOW TO TRY IT (ABOUT TWO MINUTES)
Option 1, by hand:
1. On first launch, choose "Set up by hand".
2. Add two or three error types, for example "Wrong Response" and "Track Skipped".
3. On the Log screen, tap a tile. The count rises and an undo bar appears. Tap Undo once to see it.
4. Add a note, or use a note template.
5. Open Review to see the day's entries in time order. Edit or delete one.
6. Share the log: the share sheet opens with the plain-text log.

Option 2, with the demo setup file:
1. In Safari on the review device, open https://iainchesworthlabs.github.io/icl-error-journaling/demo/demo-setup.ejconfig
2. Open the file in CS Bingo Board, then choose "Use this setup". The board fills with 11 error types, a location (North Site) and note templates.
3. Continue from step 3 above.
The same file is attached to this submission.

IPAD
On iPad the app works in portrait and landscape. In landscape the board and the day's log sit side by side.
