**Dictate a note with the microphone.** The note field has a microphone at its trailing edge (the left in a right-to-left layout). Tap it and speak: the words appear in the note as you say them, and a second tap, a minute of silence or leaving the app ends it. Recognition is the phone's own and happens on the phone (iPhone and iPad: on-device speech recognition; Android 13 and later: the on-device recogniser), so no sound or text is sent anywhere, and the microphone is not shown where the phone cannot do that. The microphone and speech permissions are asked for at the first tap. Not yet run on a device.
