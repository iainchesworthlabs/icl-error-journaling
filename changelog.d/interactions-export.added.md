**Interactions can be shared and copied.** A log with interaction entries now shares, copies and re-shares: each interaction is a line of its own, `10:12 (Interaction) Spoke with A.S.`, with no `T|` marker so the ticket indexer does not read it, and the error lines are unchanged. A person is written as initials by default, in Share, Copy and Copy log alike; the choice (`full`, `initials`, `someone`) is stored as the preference `name_export` and has its Settings row in the Settings package of the interactions board. Review and the board show the full name. The list of people is never exported. Not yet run on a device.
