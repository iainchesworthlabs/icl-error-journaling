**A long word on a tile fits its label on macOS and iOS.** The tile shrank a long word such as "Disconnection" by the ratio of the widths, but the system font spaces smaller text more loosely, so on macOS the word came out a little wider than the label. The fit now measures the word at the size it reaches and corrects twice, and the smallest-size check uses the measured width. Not yet run on a device.
