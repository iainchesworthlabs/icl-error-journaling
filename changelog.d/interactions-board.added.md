**The Interactions board works.** With **Second board: Interactions** on in Settings, the board now has tiles out of the box: Spoke with, Played song, Visitor interaction, Gave item, Greeted and Helped (a setup file with `interaction_types` replaces them). Double-tap a tile to log it, as on the Errors board; the count badge, the undo message and the hold-to-add-a-typed-note all work the same, and the Other tile lists the types that are not on the board. A tile whose line has a **[person]** slot, such as Spoke with, opens a list of people as soon as it is logged: type a name once, tick one or several people (up to 20, or **Everyone here** for the people of the place in force), tap **Done**, and pick them from the list next time. **Skip** leaves the entry as logged. A tile with an answer, such as Played song, asks for it in a small sheet. The entry is in the log before either sheet opens, so nothing is lost if you skip or close them. Names are kept on the phone only. Not yet run on a device; Share and Copy of a log that holds an interaction come with the export package, and Review does not edit interactions yet.
