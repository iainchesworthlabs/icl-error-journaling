**The launch screen shows the Log board.** While CS Bingo Board starts, the iPhone now shows the shape of the board (the header card, the note box, the Last bar, the 12 tiles and the tab bar, in plain grey) in light and dark, where it showed a plain yellow screen, so the app appears to open straight onto the board. The picture is drawn from the app's own screen layout, and a check fails the build if the two drift apart.
